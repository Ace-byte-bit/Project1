About Isoko Digital

Isoko Digital is a source — a wellspring of trusted digital life for Rwanda. A place where news, bookmarks, business and community come together under one roof.

Our Mission

We believe Rwandans deserve a digital space that reflects their country — multilingual, mobile-first, and built by locals for locals. Isoko Digital is our answer: a lightweight, accessible platform that works on any device, anywhere in Rwanda.

Our Story

Isoko Digital started as a personal project — a way to learn web development while building something useful for Rwanda. What began as a single news page has grown into a multi-section platform, hand-built with care and no shortcuts.

Every feature you see here was built from scratch: the animated gradients, the news system, the bookmark manager, the signup wizard. No frameworks, no templates — just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
