Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Your privacy matters. This page explains — in plain language — what data Isoko Digital collects, how we use it, and what control you have.

1. What We Collect

Isoko Digital stores the following data only in your browser (via localStorage):

  • Account info — your name and email when you sign up
  • Bookmarks — links you save on the Bookmark page
  • Likes & saves — articles you interact with

None of this data is sent to any server. It stays on your device and belongs to you.

2. What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We don't sell your data
  • ❌ We don't track you across other websites
  • ❌ We don't run advertising trackers
  • ❌ We don't use your data for anything except the features you see

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We don't use cookies. We use localStorage — a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Clearing your browser data will erase everything Isoko Digital has stored about you.

4. Third Parties

Two external services help the site work:

  • Google Favicons — shows site icons on the Bookmark page. Google may log the domain you're fetching.
  • Your hosting provider — serves the pages. They may collect basic visitor logs (IP address, browser type).

No personal data from your Isoko Digital account is shared with either.

5. Your Rights

Because all data lives in your browser, you're always in control:

  • See your data — open DevTools → Application → Local Storage
  • Delete your data — clear your browser storage
  • Port your data — copy the localStorage entries to your own file

6. Children

Isoko Digital is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@isokodigital.rw.
